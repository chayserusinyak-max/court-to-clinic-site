Swimming Deep Dive: Streamlined Power

Swimming rewards connected movement. Propulsion begins with the kick, travels through the hips and trunk, and completes with long, efficient strokes from the shoulders to the fingertips. Disruption anywhere along the line slows velocity and increases drag.

Movement Snapshot

The body stays long and aligned while the hips roll gently with each stroke. Core stability keeps the ribs and pelvis balanced so the catch phase can anchor and drive the body forward.

Common Watchpoints

  • Shoulder impingement surfaces with repeated overhead reach under fatigue.
  • Lumbar hyperextension discomfort grows with aggressive dolphin kicks.
  • Knee stress pops up during breaststroke turns and whip kicks.

Training Emphasis

  • Pair kickboard intervals with ankle mobility to boost flutter efficiency.
  • Integrate dryland pillar circuits linking ribcage control with hip stability.
  • Use resistance-band scapular retraction and depression drills to support smooth shoulder glide.

Return to the Movement Lab anytime to tailor accessory work, interval structure, or tissue care around your stroke goals.
